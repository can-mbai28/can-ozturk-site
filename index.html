Case study · Kellogg × Replit Hackathon · October 2026

La Cocinita
Catering Ops

A back of house app for a family restaurant group that turns a catering order into exact prep quantities and live packing checklists for the kitchen and the food truck.

Role Product lead, PRD authorClient La Cocinita, EvanstonBuilt with ReplitDemo Nov 3, 2026

The problem

La Cocinita runs two restaurants and three food trucks and does a lot of catering. Their planning tool was a spreadsheet: type in a taco bar for 35 and it returns ounces of meat and sauce.

Everything else lived in people's heads. Which orders need sporks and which don't, how many serving tongs, what goes on the truck, how to handle the one in ten customers who wants all brisket or 100 utensils, and which orders have a celiac guest.

What we built

Order intakePaste an ezCater order or upload a screenshot. AI drafts the event, the manager reviews and approves it.
Rules enginePortions, disposables and serving gear per package, in a grid the manager edits like their old spreadsheet.
Live checklistsPrep, pack and load the truck on a kitchen tablet. Several people can check items at once and see who did what.
Daily and weekly totalsPrep totals across every order for the day, a truck load view, and a weekly purchase list.
Celiac flagShows on every screen for that order so the kitchen never misses it.
English and SpanishOne tap switches the kitchen screens to Spanish.

Product calls I made

  • AI drafts, the rules engine does the math.The model reads messy orders well but shouldn't invent quantities. It fills the form, and every number comes from rules the owner controls.
  • Serving gear scales by party size, not per guest.The owner listed 5 tongs "per person." Taken literally that is 250 tongs for 50 guests, so we treat it as one set per 50 guests.
  • Simple live sync over full version control.Our early flows had drafts, diffs and conflict resolution. The real need was several people checking items at once, so each tap saves on its own and edited items get a "changed, recheck" flag.
  • Every feature ranked P0, P1 or P2.With a three day build window, the PRD told the builder exactly what to cut first, plus 44 edge cases and phase by phase prompts for Replit.

Case study · Kellogg MBAi Hackathon · September 2026

Kompass

An app that helps Kellogg students actually meet their class: find the classmates they have real things in common with, meet them in person, and keep those friendships from fading.

Team Team PioneersBuilt with ClaudeHackathon Sep 26, 2026
Try the click-through demo →

The problem

Kellogg students have two years with hundreds of classmates, yet most only really get to know their own section.

Directories are intimidatingScrolling hundreds of names doesn't tell you who you'd actually click with.
Events are a coin flipYou show up not knowing which of your people will be there.
Follow-ups fade"We should grab coffee" rarely turns into coffee.

What we built

FindYour class is one bubble that shrinks as you filter by program, career, section, clubs, KWEST trip and hobbies, then pops into real people.
Know why to say hiEvery profile shows what you have in common and what they're open to chatting about, so there's always an opener.
Meet in real lifeSee which events the people on your list are going to, then scan each other's code to unlock full profiles.
After the eventA five second step to mark who you met, then privately sort them into Keep in touch or Nice to meet.
Follow-up agentNotices when a friendship goes quiet and drafts a message for you to send, edit, snooze or skip.
Trust and privacyPhotos and contact info unlock only in person, notes stay private, and anyone can be reported to a Kellogg safety team.

The app

The agentic part

  • Watches your Keep in touch group every day.No prompt needed. It only looks at people you chose to stay in touch with.
  • Reads your last conversation."Let's meet up after trivia night…" and then nothing for 16 days.
  • Decides who's overdue.14 or more days of quiet, three people at a time, so it never floods you.
  • Drafts one friendly message with one reason, in your voice.You choose to send, edit, snooze or let it go. It never sends on its own.

Powered by Claude with tool use. Works offline with built-in drafts.
